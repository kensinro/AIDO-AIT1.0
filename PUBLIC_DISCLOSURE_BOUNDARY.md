# Public disclosure boundary

## Public
- minimum reference implementation;
- synthetic example;
- tests for the public reference lifecycle;
- manuscript calibration summary;
- hash-only provenance index;
- identity/hash of the historical evaluated implementation.

## Controlled reviewer access
- exact historical v5.8.1 evaluated source archive;
- fresh 49-test verification record;
- detailed recursive self-audit / Challenger / regression reports.

## Private
- later development branches;
- advanced rule libraries and orchestration;
- internal experience ledgers;
- unpublished cases;
- commercial/product integration;
- AIT 2.0 capabilities.

The public tier is designed to prove existence and inspectability of the
published MVP without transferring the complete development know-how.
